## Evidence and outcome

and success metric. Use sanitized evidence; do not copy production logs or caller
data. Distinguish measured facts from hypotheses.

## Bounded change

Define scope, non-goals, preserved invariants, dependencies, compatibility costs,
and resource ceiling. Link the [verification contract](AGENTIC_VERIFICATION_LOOP.md).

## Acceptance and follow-up

Record actual checks, metric changes, remaining limitations and reviewer decision.
Promote durable findings into tests/docs/ADRs/memory. State who rechecks the outcome
and at which milestone; an unmeasured expectation is not an improvement claim.
